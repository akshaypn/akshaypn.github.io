Selected research & releases

Public work

Each release reflects a team effort. The notes below describe my contribution within the wider research and engineering work.

Data · October 2025

Read release ↗

QVAC Genesis I

A public multi-domain educational synthetic dataset for LLM pretraining, built around a learning-from-failures methodology.

Contribution: DevOps and MLOps for data generation and model training, distributed infrastructure, pretraining ablations, and release analysis.

  • 41B public synthetic tokens
  • 9 educational domains
  • Downstream and model-based evaluation

Data · December 2025

Read release ↗

QVAC Genesis II

An expansion of Genesis that adds option-level reasoning and a dual-method data-generation pipeline.

Contribution: Data and evaluation pipeline work, distributed training infrastructure, ablations, and analysis supporting the public release.

  • 148B tokens combined with Genesis I
  • 19 educational domains
  • 107B new tokens across 86M samples

Edge training · December 2025

Read release ↗

QVAC Fabric LoRA Fine-Tuning

A generalized, edge-first LoRA fine-tuning workflow for heterogeneous GPUs in the llama.cpp ecosystem.

Contribution: Original proof of concept, dataset curation, experimental design, Vulkan-versus-PyTorch validation, and benchmark methodology review.

  • Hardware-agnostic workflow
  • Mobile-GPU fine-tuning
  • Qwen3 and Gemma 3 support

Low-bit systems · March 2026

Read release ↗

BitNet b1.58 Edge Fine-Tuning

Cross-platform LoRA fine-tuning and inference for BitNet models on heterogeneous edge GPUs.

Contribution: Low-bit benchmarking, implementation review, quality evaluation, mobile and desktop testing, and research-paper development.

  • Up to 13B parameters fine-tuned on an iPhone 16
  • 1B model fine-tuned on a Samsung S25
  • 2.1–11.3× faster edge-GPU inference than CPU

Health AI · May 2026

Read release ↗

QVAC MedPsy

Compact medical language models designed for private local deployment, with multi-stage post-training and mobile-focused quantization research.

Contribution: Credited contributor to the public release and technical representative for the research.

  • 1.7B and 4B model releases
  • Quantization studied for mobile deployment
  • Seven closed-ended medical benchmarks
